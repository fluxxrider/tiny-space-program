About this film

In April 2025, Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland and Romeo Dean of the AI Futures Project published AI 2027, a month-by-month scenario for how superhuman AI could arrive, and two ways it could end.

This unofficial explainer follows the scenario from the first stumbling agents in 2025 to the committee vote in October 2027 and both of its endings. Every frame is rendered live in your browser, and the original score was synthesized in code.

Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys skip five seconds, and F toggles fullscreen.
